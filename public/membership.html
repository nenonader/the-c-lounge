Private Clarities

Before the Invitation

The relationship is personal. The terms are precise. These are the questions most often resolved before a private consultation.

01How does membership begin?

Every relationship begins with a confidential application or private introduction. The desk reviews the required level of support, geographic scope and preferred way of working before proposing the appropriate arrangement.

02What separates the three tiers?

Signature is designed around individual travel support. Privé extends across family-office aviation and lifestyle requirements. Sovereign creates a standing mandate for principals, executive teams and complex global movement.

03Are fees published?

No. Scope, usage and service structure are different for every member. Fees, supplier costs and any minimum commitments are stated privately and confirmed in writing before activation.

04Does membership guarantee every request?

Membership creates priority, continuity and one accountable desk. Supplier availability, regulation and local conditions still apply; the desk confirms what is secured before any commitment is treated as final.

05Can the arrangement cover a family or office?

Yes, where agreed. Privé and Sovereign can be structured around authorised travellers, family-office teams or executive groups with clear contact, approval and confidentiality protocols.

06How is discretion protected?

Information is limited to the people and vetted suppliers required to fulfil a request. Sensitive details, authorised contacts and communication channels are agreed at the beginning of the relationship.
