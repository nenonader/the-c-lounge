The Difference

The Client Sees a Journey.
The Desk Sees Every Dependency.

A change to the aircraft changes the slot. The slot changes the meet-and-assist window. That changes ground positioning, residence arrival, residence timing and the evening programme. A catalogue sells each item. A private office protects the whole sequence.

T–72H · 22:10Private Office

The Brief Arrives Once

Route, travellers, preferences, sensitivities, authorised contacts and decision rights are captured in one private record. The desk separates what must be known from what must remain restricted.

  • Named owner for the entire movement
  • Single source of truth
  • Communication protocol agreed
T–70H · 00:05Discipline 02

The Mission Defines the Aircraft

Range, runway, passenger profile, luggage, cabin expectations and onward timing are evaluated together. Suitable options are verified before they are presented.

  • Aircraft and operator review
  • Routing and slot dependencies
  • Primary and contingency plan
T–48H · 09:20Discipline 01

Arrival Begins Before Touchdown

Meet-and-assist, immigration, baggage, lounge and ramp-side permissions are aligned with the live flight plan. The receiving team knows the vehicle, authorised contact and privacy protocol.

  • Arrival window monitored
  • Border process coordinated
  • Vehicle handoff pre-identified
T–36H · 15:40Discipline 03

The Door Opens at the Right Moment

Driver, vehicle, route, luggage capacity, arrival protocol and fallback access are confirmed as one transfer. Delays update the whole ground sequence, not only the pickup time.

  • Driver identity verified
  • Live route and access review
  • Backup vehicle positioned where required
T–30H · 18:15Discipline 04

The Room Is Ready Before It Is Needed

Room allocation, privacy, companion rooms, dietary notes, access names and late-arrival procedure are checked directly. The destination receives only the details required to prepare correctly.

  • Preference profile transmitted selectively
  • Arrival and access reconfirmed
  • On-property escalation contact held
T–12H · 08:30Discipline 05

Time Is Returned, Not Merely Scheduled

Dining, gifting, personal shopping and last-minute private requests are placed inside the live movement plan. Nothing is arranged in isolation from location, access or timing.

  • Preferences carried forward
  • Reservations aligned to movement
  • Changes resolved through the same desk
T–04H · 16:10Discipline 07

The Moment Includes Every Threshold

Invitation, entrance protocol, guest list, transfer window, host contact and departure plan are treated as one experience. The desk knows what happens before the door and after it closes.

  • Access terms confirmed
  • Arrival protocol rehearsed
  • Exit movement protected
D+01 · 11:30Discipline 06

Land and Water Share One Clock

Yacht, tender, marina access, luggage, guest arrival and shore transport are sequenced around weather and local conditions. A marine change updates the shore plan immediately.

  • Vessel and operator checks
  • Weather-sensitive timing
  • Shore-side continuity
CONTINUOUSDiscipline 09

The Relationship Outlives the Itinerary

With an agreed standing arrangement, approved preferences, contacts and ways of working remain available for the next movement. The value is continuity: less explanation, fewer handoffs, faster judgement.

  • Preferences retained by agreement
  • Named senior contact
  • Post-journey review and refinement
